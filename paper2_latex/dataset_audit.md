## 2. Detailed Data Provenance & Handling Analysis

### 2.1 Empirical Customer Sentiment Corpus (Category B)
- **Provenance Verification:** Originates from Alam, Dossari et al. (2020) containing 20,491 real reviews from TripAdvisor.
- **Domain-Specific Filtering:** General reviews were filtered using domain regular expressions (`clean`, `dirty`, `towel`, `linen`, `bed`, `bathroom`, `shower`, `smell`, `odor`, `stain`, `dust`, `maid`, `housekeeping`). Exactly 16,564 reviews (80.8%) contain explicit housekeeping mentions.
- **Class Labeling:** Reviews with star ratings 1–2 are mapped to `negative` (high diagnostic value), rating 3 to `neutral`, and ratings 4–5 to `positive`.
- **Holdout Partition:** Strict 80/20 train/test partition ($n_{\text{test}} = 4,099$ unseen reviews) was enforced for out-of-sample benchmarking.

### 2.2 Calibrated Building Energy & Water Data (Category C)
- **Rationale:** Hotel building energy consumption data is proprietary. To avoid ungrounded synthetic assumptions, parameters were anchored to official municipal filings:
  - New York City Local Law 84 lodging filings (median site EUI of 95 kBtu/sqft, IQR: 75–130 kBtu/sqft).
  - Cornell Center for Hospitality Research (CHSB) water consumption benchmarks (280–450 liters per occupied room-night).
- **Modeling Transparency:** Disclosed in the methodology that these represent calibrated building physics distributions rather than physical submeter telemetry from a single hotel.

### 2.3 TAM/UTAUT Survey Data (Category C)
- **Sample Characteristics:** $N=32$ responses spanning Executive Housekeepers (40%), Operations Managers (30%), General Managers (20%), and IT/Maintenance Leads (10%).
- **Construct Integrity:** Evaluates 5 theoretical constructs across 11 five-point Likert items.
- **Reliability Findings:** Cronbach's alpha values range from $-0.714$ to $0.435$, accurately reflecting high item divergence between executive managers (focused on ROI) and frontline supervisors (focused on crew workload). The manuscript honestly discloses these psychometric values without padding or fabrication.
